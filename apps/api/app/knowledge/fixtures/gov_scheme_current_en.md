# One Stop Centre (OSC) Scheme Guidelines (Current Version 2.0)
**Ministry of Women and Child Development, Government of India**
*Published: 2022-04-01 | Effective: 2022-04-01 to Present | Jurisdiction: INDIA | Tier: 1*

## Section 1: Objective and Scope
The One Stop Centre (OSC) scheme facilitates access to an integrated range of services including medical aid, police assistance, legal counseling, shelter, and psycho-social support to women affected by violence, both in private and public spaces under one roof.

## Section 2: Shelter and Accommodation
Subject to availability and basic intake identification:
1. Temporary shelter shall be provided for a maximum period of 5 days (120 hours) within the One Stop Centre premises.
2. For long-term shelter beyond 5 days, the Centre Administrator shall refer the aggrieved woman to registered Swadhar Greh or Ujjawala homes in coordination with the District Child Protection Officer (DCPO) or District Social Welfare Officer (DSWO).
3. Exception: No woman shall be turned away during nocturnal hours (8:00 PM to 6:00 AM) even if capacity is temporarily constrained; basic emergency bedding must be arranged.

## Section 3: Legal Aid and Police Assistance
1. A panel advocate from the District Legal Services Authority (DLSA) shall be made available at no cost to the aggrieved woman.
2. Protection Officers appointed under the Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act 2005 (PWDVA) shall assist in filing Domestic Incident Reports (DIR) before the Judicial Magistrate.
3. Provided that police intervention shall require informed consent unless there is imminent threat to life or severe grievous hurt.
