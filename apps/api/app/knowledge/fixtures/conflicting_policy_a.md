# Regional Welfare Advisory Circular A (Directive 2024/01)
**State Social Welfare Board**
*Published: 2024-01-10 | Effective: 2024-01-10 to Present | Jurisdiction: INDIA | Tier: 2*

## Section 1: Emergency Shelter Duration Limit
Emergency shelter stays in district crisis facilities shall be strictly capped at 7 days. Extension beyond 7 days requires formal judicial magistrate authorization or written DSWO clearance.
