# Standard Operating Procedures for Women Helpline 181 and NHAA 14566 (Version 3.0)
**Ministry of Women and Child Development & National Health Authority, Government of India**
*Published: 2023-06-01 | Effective: 2023-06-01 to Present | Jurisdiction: INDIA | Tier: 1*

## Section 1: Emergency Triage and Response Tiers
Helpline counselors must categorize incoming distress calls into three operational tiers:
- Tier A (Immediate Threat / Weapon Present / Active Physical Harm): Instant conference with emergency police dispatch (112) and dispatch of mobile emergency van within 15 minutes.
- Tier B (Domestic Harassment / Severe Coercion / Imminent Eviction): Triage within 1 hour, allocation of Protection Officer or OSC legal counselor.
- Tier C (Information Request / Institutional Inquiries / Follow-up): Response within 24 hours.

## Section 2: Privacy and Caller Consent Protocol
1. Caller location and masked contact numbers shall never be disclosed to third parties without recorded verbal consent, unless the caller is incapacitated or life is in imminent peril.
2. In accordance with Section 12 of the PWDVA 2005, the helpline shall facilitate filing of Domestic Incident Reports (DIR) with the local magistrate having competent territorial jurisdiction.
