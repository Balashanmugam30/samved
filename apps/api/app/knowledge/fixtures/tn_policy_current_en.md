# Government of Tamil Nadu Women Shelter and Protection Guidelines (Version 2.1)
**Department of Social Welfare and Women Empowerment, Government of Tamil Nadu**
*Published: 2023-01-15 | Effective: 2023-01-15 to Present | Jurisdiction: TAMIL_NADU | Tier: 1*

## Section 1: Objective and Emergency Support
To provide immediate safe shelter, nutritious meals, legal guidance, and medical assistance to women facing domestic violence, harassment, or acute physical threats in Tamil Nadu.

## Section 2: Short-Stay Home Admission
1. Under the supervision of the District Social Welfare Officer (DSWO) or All Women Police Stations (AWPS), aggrieved women and their minor children are entitled to emergency shelter for up to 7 days free of cost.
2. In acute crisis situations, admission shall be granted immediately for the initial 48 hours without demanding identity proofs or prior formal complaints.
3. Exception: Aggrieved individuals suffering from severe substance withdrawal or psychiatric crises shall be admitted in coordination with designated government medical college hospitals.

## Section 3: Legal Aid and 181 Coordination
1. Free legal counsel is assigned through the Tamil Nadu State Legal Services Authority (TNSLSA) within 24 hours of intake.
2. The Tamil Nadu 181 Women Helpline coordinates emergency ambulance and rescue vehicles statewide.
