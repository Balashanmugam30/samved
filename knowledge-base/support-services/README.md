# Knowledge Base — Support Services Directory

Contains verified directories of government-recognized rehabilitation, medical, and legal assistance facilities.

## Resource Categories
- **IRCAs (Integrated Rehabilitation Centres for Addicts)**: State, district, address, bed capacity, contact credentials.
- **Government Hospital Psychiatry & De-addiction Wards**: Emergency detox beds, nodal officers.
- **District Legal Services Authorities (DLSA)**: Legal aid and representation under Legal Services Authorities Act.
- **One Stop Centres (OSC)** & Women Helplines: For intersectional gender-based violence cases.
