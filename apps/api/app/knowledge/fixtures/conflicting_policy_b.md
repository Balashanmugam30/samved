# Regional Welfare Advisory Circular B (Directive 2024/08)
**Metropolitan Welfare Board**
*Published: 2024-02-15 | Effective: 2024-02-15 to Present | Jurisdiction: INDIA | Tier: 2*

## Section 1: Emergency Shelter Duration Limit
Emergency shelter stays in district crisis facilities must be provided for a minimum duration of 14 days and mandatory co-signature by a recognized guardian or gazetted officer is required at admission.
