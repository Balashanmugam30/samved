# Knowledge Base — Government Welfare & Rehabilitation Schemes

Contains structured documentation and eligibility guidelines for official rehabilitation schemes under the Government of India.

## Key Target Schemes
1. **National Action Plan for Drug Demand Reduction (NAPDDR)**:
   - Administered by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment (MoSJE).
   - Financial support for awareness generation, capacity building, and rehabilitation.
2. **Integrated Rehabilitation Centres for Addicts (IRCAs)**:
   - Operating guidelines, standard treatment protocols, and beneficiary entitlements.
3. **Community-based Peer Led Intervention (CPLI)** and **Outreach and Drop-In Centres (ODIC)**.
4. **Nasha Mukt Bharat Abhiyaan (NMBA)**: District-level community interventions.
