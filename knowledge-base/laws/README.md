# Knowledge Base — Authoritative Laws & Statutes

This directory stores verified statutory texts and gazette notifications governing substance use, rehabilitation, and victim rights in India.

## Key Target Statutes
1. **Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act, 1985 (NDPS)**:
   - Section 64A: Immunity from prosecution to addicts volunteering for treatment.
   - Section 71: Power of Government to establish centres for identification, treatment, education, after-care, rehabilitation of addicts.
2. **Mental Healthcare Act, 2017**:
   - Section 115: Presumption of severe stress in case of attempt to die by suicide; ban on criminalization under IPC/BNS.
   - Right to access mental health care services funded by relevant governments.
3. **Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 (BNS)**: Relevant statutory references replacing former IPC sections.
