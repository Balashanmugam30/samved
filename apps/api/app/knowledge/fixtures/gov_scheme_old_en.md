# One Stop Centre Pilot Scheme Guidelines (Archived Version 1.0)
**Ministry of Women and Child Development, Government of India**
*Published: 2015-01-01 | Effective: 2015-01-01 to 2022-03-31 | Jurisdiction: INDIA | Tier: 1 | Status: SUPERSEDED*

## Section 1: Pilot Scope
The pilot phase of the One Stop Crisis Centre was restricted to selected tier-1 district headquarters on a trial basis.

## Section 2: Shelter Restrictions
1. Temporary shelter was strictly capped at 3 days (72 hours).
2. Swadhar Greh transfers required an upfront non-encumbrance affidavit before admission.
3. Note: This version is superseded by Version 2.0 (effective April 2022).
